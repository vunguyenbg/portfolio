How it plays

Blockers don't stop you. They fly.

In Arrows, CubeAway and other arrow puzzles a blocked piece is a dead end. Chain Slide flips that rule.

Try it Tap the pink block
  1. Tap a blockIt slides in the direction of its arrow.
  2. Push the whole rowEvery block in its path gets knocked off the board. One good tap clears 3–5 blocks.
  3. Free the stuck onesA block facing a rock can't move. Another block has to knock it out.
  4. Clear the board in limited tapsPlayers hunt for the tap that clears the most.

Pitch

Why Chain Slide

Market
Arrow puzzles are among 2026's fastest-growing hybrid-casual lines. Versions with boosters, lives and LiveOps (CubeAway, Maze Out) reached the top 200 grossing charts.
Hook
A familiar arrow read with a new rule: the blocker gets pushed out instead of stopping you. Combos and a clear "aha" when a stuck block is knocked free.
Ad creative
"1 tap, 6 blocks gone" reads in under two seconds. The stuck-block rescue makes a second hook.
Monetization
Extra taps on fail (IAP and rewarded ad), undo and hint boosters, lives, a toy-collection meta and a LiveOps calendar.
Status
Playable web prototype with 7 solver-verified levels. Next: a Unity build with 30–40 levels and final art in 2–3 weeks, ready for publisher SDK integration.
