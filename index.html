New concept · 2026

Chain Slide

A hybrid-casual arrow puzzle built around one twist: blockers don't stop you, they fly.

Playable prototype · 7 levels Unity build in progress

Tap a block and it slides along its arrow, knocking every block in its path off the board.

Goal
Clear the board within a limited number of taps. Rocks stop a chain, so players plan which tap clears the most.
Hook
A block stuck against a rock can't move on its own. Another block has to knock it free.
Monetization
Extra taps on fail (IAP and rewarded ad), undo and hint boosters, lives, a toy-collection meta and a LiveOps calendar.
Market
Arrow puzzles are among 2026's fastest-growing hybrid-casual lines (Arrows, CubeAway, Maze Out).
