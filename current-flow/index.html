Current Flow

How it plays

You don't move the fish. You move the water.

Escape puzzles like Bus Jam and Arrows ask you to tap each piece out. Here one turned tile reroutes a whole school, so players plan the route before they release.

  1. Read the reefEvery tile carries a current. Arrows show where the water flows.
  2. Turn the currentsTap a tile to turn its current 90°. A dotted line shows where each school will swim, with a green ring or red cross at the end.
  3. ReleaseAll schools swim at once. Wrong gate, rock or loop and the level fails. Current changes are limited.

Pitch

Why Chain Slide

Market
Hybrid-casual puzzles are the fastest-growing casual segment in 2026. Pipe and flow puzzles are a crowded but small-scale space with no hybrid-casual leader yet.
Hook
Players steer the environment instead of single pieces. Schools merging into one current and pouring into a gate is the payoff moment.
Ad creative
A whole school sweeping through the reef and into its gate, or getting swept into the wrong one.
Monetization
Extra current changes on fail (IAP and rewarded ad), a net booster, lives, and an aquarium meta for collecting the fish that make it home.
Status
Early mechanic test: a 3D web prototype with 3 levels. Readability is solved with predicted routes; next step is a creative test before a Unity build.
