How it plays

A blocked penguin is not a dead end

In most arrow puzzles a piece stuck behind another is a dead end. Here the slider shoves the whole line ahead of it, so one smart tap can splash five or six penguins at once.

  1. TapThe penguin belly-slides the way it faces.
  2. PushEvery penguin in its path gets shoved along.
  3. SplashPenguins pushed off the floe swim to your island and grow your colony.

Pitch

Why Penguin Slide

Market
Arrow and tap-away puzzles keep charting; most use plain blocks or cars.
Hook
Chain pushes: one tap can clear a whole row, a moment that is easy to read in one second.
Ad creative
Opening shot: a single tap sends a line of penguins splashing into the sea.
Monetization
Rewarded hints and undo, limited taps, colony meta (collect rare penguins, decorate the island).
Status
Playable web prototype with 7 levels and a solver-based level generator. Unity build in 2–3 weeks.
